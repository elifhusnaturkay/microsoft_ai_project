# Tuition, Fees, and Cost of Attendance

**Source:** SHSU Cost of Attendance
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/cost-aid/cost-attendance

Cost of Attendance (COA) is the estimated cost to attend Sam Houston State University for an academic year, including tuition and fees plus basic living expenses. The average cost of tuition and fees for a typical student is based on 30 credit hours per year (15 in fall, 15 in spring). Figures below are 9-month academic year estimates for Fall 2026/Spring 2027 (revised 03/01/26); actual costs vary by program and housing/dining choices.

**Full-Time Undergraduate Non-Resident (includes international students) — 2026-2027:**
- Tuition and Fees: $23,810
- Food and Housing: $12,804
- Books and Supplies: $1,380
- Transportation: $2,004
- Miscellaneous/Personal: $1,792
- Loan Fees: $70
- **Total Budget: $41,860**

**Full-Time Graduate Non-Resident — 2026-2027:**
- Tuition and Fees: $18,048
- Food and Housing: $12,804
- Books and Supplies: $920
- Transportation: $2,004
- Miscellaneous/Personal: $1,792
- Loan Fees: $120
- **Total Budget: $35,688**

**Per Semester Credit Hour (2026-2027):**
- Resident students: $50 tuition + $194 designated tuition = $244/credit hour
- Nonresident/international students: $455 tuition + $194 designated tuition = $649/credit hour

**Additional mandatory fees (per credit hour):**
- Institutional Services Fee: $80
- Student Service Fee: $17.60 (fall/spring), $8.80 (summer)
- Intercollegiate Athletic Fee: $20 (fall/spring), $10 (summer)

**Fixed semester fees:**
- Student Center Fee: $100
- Medical Fee: $75
- Recreational Sports Fee: up to $100
- International student fee: $75/semester (for services provided through the Office of International Programs)

**Payment and installment plans:** Students may pay tuition and fees in full or by installment plan for fall, spring, and summer semesters (terms published on the Student Account Services website). Installment plan participants may have different refund schedules and could owe more in tuition and fees than already collected if they withdraw. Accepted payment methods: cash, check, money order (in person); e-check, wire transfer, major credit cards (online).

**Important:** A student who fails to make full payment of tuition and mandatory fees by the due date may be prohibited from registering for classes until full payment is made.
