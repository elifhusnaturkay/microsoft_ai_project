# Financial / Budget Guide for International Students

**Source:** SHSU Cost of Attendance
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/cost-aid/cost-attendance

Full-Time Undergraduate Non-Resident (includes international students) estimated 9-month academic year budget for 2026-2027: Tuition and Fees $23,810, Food and Housing $12,804, Books and Supplies $1,380, Transportation $2,004, Miscellaneous/Personal $1,792, Loan Fees $70 — **Total Budget: $41,860**. This is also the minimum bank balance figure typically required to demonstrate financial support for the I-20/F-1 visa process.

---

**Source:** SHSU Undergraduate Catalog — Tuition & Fees
**URL:** https://catalog.shsu.edu/undergraduate/financial-information/tuition-fees/

Nonresident/international students pay $455 tuition + $194 designated tuition = $649 per semester credit hour, plus additional per-credit fees (Institutional Services Fee $80, Student Service Fee $17.60, Athletic Fee $20) and fixed semester fees (Student Center Fee $100, Medical Fee $75, Recreational Sports Fee up to $100, International student fee $75/semester). Installment payment plans are available for fall, spring, and summer semesters.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — application/visa fee steps
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/

Pre-arrival mandatory fees encountered during the application/visa process: ApplyTexas application fee $90 (step1/step3); SEVIS I-901 fee $350 (step8); MRV visa application fee ~$185 (step10). Combined required government/application fees before arrival: approximately $625.

**Source:** SHSU Residence Life — Meal Plans
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/offices-departments/residence-life/living-on-campus/meal-plans/meal-plans

On-campus meal plan costs per semester range from $635 (Bearkat Lite, commuter) to $2,750 (All Access, required default for first-year residents). See docs/bearkat_life.md for the full breakdown of all six meal plan tiers.

**Source:** SHSU International Student Health Insurance (Academic Health Plans / myahpcare)
**URL:** https://shsu-intl.myahpcare.com/waiver

International students are required to carry the university Student Health Insurance Plan unless they submit a waiver showing comparable existing coverage.

**Source:** Cost of Living in Huntsville, TX
**URL:** https://livingcost.org/cost/united-states/tx/huntsville

Estimated monthly costs for a single person in Huntsville, TX: 1-bedroom apartment (city center) $1,038, 1-bedroom apartment (cheaper area) $914, 3-bedroom apartment (city center) $1,637, utilities (single person) $76/month, internet (50+ Mbps) $59.60/month. Groceries: milk $0.79/L, bread $1.10 (0.5kg), eggs $4.02/dozen, chicken breast $6.65/kg. Transportation: local transit ticket $1.96, monthly transit pass $60.60, gasoline $0.85/L. Dining out: lunch menu $20.10, fast food meal $11.60, coffee $5.84. Overall total monthly cost with rent for one person: approximately $1,900; without rent: approximately $826.

**Source:** SHSU Cost and Financial Aid — Bearkat Emergency Fund / Bearkats Helping Bearkats
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/cost-aid/bearkats-helping-bearkats

The Bearkat Emergency Fund provides temporary financial assistance to students facing emergency situations such as sudden medical expenses, loss of income, or food insecurity, helping them stay focused on their education. Requests are reviewed by a committee considering the student's expenses and available resources. The related Bearkat Assistance Program gives enrolled students quick, personalized, and confidential access to SHSU staff who can connect them with relevant resources. Donations can be made by selecting "Bearkat Emergency Fund" as the gift designation.
