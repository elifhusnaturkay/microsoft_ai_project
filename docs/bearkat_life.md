# Campus Life at SHSU (Bearkat Life)

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Housing Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step20.html

New students choose between off-campus housing (SHSU provides a downloadable PDF list of recommended off-campus options) and on-campus housing, applied for through the SHSU Housing Portal (https://shsu.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalX/Login). Students are encouraged to explore available residence halls and room types before applying and to apply early to secure a place. Residence Life info: https://www.shsu.edu/dept/residence-life/gettingstarted/residence_halls.html

**Source:** SHSU Campus Life — Housing Options
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/campus-life/housing/housing-options/

**Residence halls:** Belvin-Buchanan Hall, Elliott Hall, Estill Hall, Jackson-Shaver Hall, Lone Star Hall, Piney Woods Hall, Raven Village, Sam Houston Village, San Jacinto Hall, White Hall.

**Apartment communities:** Bearkat Village Apartments, Campus Edge Apartments, Copper Village Apartments. Small Houses are also available for select students. A non-refundable $50 application fee (Mastercard, Visa, or American Express) is required for housing applications.

**Source:** SHSU Residence Life — Meal Plans
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/offices-departments/residence-life/living-on-campus/meal-plans/meal-plans

Students entering SHSU within 1 year of high school graduation are required to live on campus and purchase a University Meal Plan during their first year (fall and spring). All on-campus residents must have a Resident meal plan except those in Bearkat Village, Campus Edge, Copper Village Apartments, and San Jacinto Studio Apartments.

**Resident meal plans:**
- All Access/Unlimited: $2,750 + tax/semester — unlimited meals + $150 Bearkat Bucks/semester (default plan for first-year students)
- 15 Meals Per Week: $2,650 + tax/semester — 15 weekly swipes + $300 Bearkat Bucks/semester
- 10 Meals Per Week: $1,960 + tax/semester — 10 weekly swipes + $450 Bearkat Bucks/semester (sophomore and above only)

**Commuter meal plans:**
- Bearkat Value: $1,030 + tax/semester — 72 meals + $400 Bearkat Bucks/semester
- Bearkat Lite: $635 + tax/semester — 32 meals + $350 Bearkat Bucks/semester
- Bearkat Flex: $750 + tax/semester — Bearkat Bucks only, no meal swipes

Meal Swipes give access to all-you-care-to-eat dining halls (Old Main Market, General's Market). Bearkat Bucks is a pre-paid declining balance usable at campus dining locations, loaded onto the Bearkat OneCard; students can add funds online.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Bearkat OneCard
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step23.html

New students visit the Global Engagement Center upon arrival in Texas to request their Bearkat OneCard (official SHSU student ID), submitted via an online portal (https://www.shsu.edu/dept/bearkatone/Request-A-Card). Requires a passport-style headshot photo and a copy of government-issued ID (e.g., passport). A valid Bearkat OneCard is required to access residence halls and to eat on campus (Bearkat Bucks and Meal Swipes are loaded onto it).

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Bank Account Opening
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step24.html

Bank of America is recommended due to a special agreement with Turkish TEB Bank, allowing withdrawals of up to $300/day from Bank of America ATMs without commission fees. Accounts can be opened online (https://promo.bankofamerica.com/hp-oaa2/) or in person at a local branch. Required documents: passport, F-1 visa, Form I-20, proof of U.S. address (utility bill or rental agreement), and Social Security Number or proof of application.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Texas Driver's License Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step25.html

Process: (1) complete an online driving course at easytexasdriversed.com ($24.95) for a completion certificate; (2) complete the required video course at impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov for a second certificate; (3) bring both certificates to a local Texas Driver License Office to schedule the driving exam; (4) upon passing, the license is mailed within approximately 10 days. Some on-campus jobs may require a valid Texas driver's license — job seekers should verify requirements. Document list available at the Texas DPS website.

**Source:** SHSU Campus Life — BearkatHQ and Student Organizations
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/campus-life/bearkat-hq

BearkatHQ is the central platform for student organizations, events, and involvement tracking. SHSU has over 250 student organizations officially recognized through BearkatHQ, which gives them access to campus resources, funding eligibility, and event support.

**Source:** SHSU Campus Recreation

Campus Recreation offers group fitness classes, personal training, fitness workshops, certifications, weight room and cardio equipment, state-of-the-art gyms, pools, and a climbing wall. The Intramural Sports program lets students compete in various team and individual sports.
