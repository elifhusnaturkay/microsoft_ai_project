# Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering — Program Details

**Source:** SHSU Undergraduate Catalog — BS Software Engineering
**URL:** https://catalog.shsu.edu/undergraduate/colleges-academic-departments/science-and-engineering-technology/computer-science/bs-software-engineering/

The Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering is offered by the Department of Computer Science and totals **120 semester credit hours**. The degree develops skills in software design, databases, programming, systems, and communication through coursework and labs, preparing students for careers in software development.

**Core Curriculum (42 hours):**
- Component Area I (Communication): 6 hours
- Component Area II (Mathematics): 3 hours — satisfied by MATH 1420
- Component Area III (Life/Physical Science): 8 hours
- Component Area IV (Language, Philosophy, Culture): 3 hours
- Component Area V (Creative Arts): 3 hours
- Component Area VI (U.S. History): 6 hours
- Component Area VII (Political Science/Government): 6 hours
- Component Area VIII (Social/Behavioral Sciences): 3 hours
- Component Area IX (Component Area Option): 4 hours

**Degree-Specific Requirements (29 hours):**
- MATH 1420 (Calculus I): 4 hours
- MATH 1430 (Calculus II): 4 hours
- MATH 2395 (Discrete Mathematics): 3 hours
- MATH 3379 (Statistical Methods in Practice): 3 hours
- MATH Advanced Elective: 3 hours
- Life/Physical Science electives: 12 hours, selecting 12 semester credit hours from BIOL 1406, BIOL 1407, CHEM 1411, CHEM 1412, PHYS 1411, PHYS 1422 (8 of these hours also satisfy Core Curriculum Component Area III)

**Major Foundation Courses — COSC (50 hours):**
- COSC 1436, COSC 1437 (Programming Fundamentals I & II): 8 hours
- COSC 2327, 2329, 3312, 3318, 3319, 3321, 3327: 21 hours
- COSC 4314, 4318, 4319, 4320, 4329, 4347, 4349: 21 hours

**Prescribed Electives:** 6 hours of COSC or DFSC electives (at least 3 hours must be advanced/upper-level).

**General Electives:** 5 hours.

**GPA requirements:** Minimum 2.0 overall GPA in all coursework; minimum 2.0 overall major GPA in all major coursework; minimum 2.0 SHSU GPA in all coursework; minimum 2.0 SHSU major GPA in all major coursework.

**Minor:** Not required for this degree, but students may optionally add a minor (requires additional semester credit hours beyond the stated program total); any minor can be paired with this degree program.

The program is housed in the Department of Computer Science within the College of Science and Engineering Technology (COSET).
