# Visa and Immigration (F-1 Status)

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — I-20 Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step6.html

To apply for the I-20, students must show a bank statement with at least $39,110 USD available, plus a clear photo/scan of the passport information page. If funds come from someone else's account, a signed sponsor letter is required (sample template provided); if the funds are in the applicant's own account and sufficient, no sponsor letter is needed. Documents are emailed to lah050@shsu.edu with the SAM ID included.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — SEVIS Fee
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step8.html

The SEVIS (I-901) fee is $350 and must be paid before starting the visa application process, at fmjfee.com/i901fee/index.html, using the SEVIS ID number found on the I-20. The payment confirmation receipt must be retained for the visa appointment.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — DS-160 Form
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step9.html

The DS-160 Nonimmigrant Visa Application is completed at https://ceac.state.gov/genniv/. Visa type is F-1. SHSU's address is 1905 University Ave, Huntsville, TX 77340, USA. The application ID number must be saved immediately, and the confirmation page must be downloaded and retained for the interview.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Visa Appointment Scheduling
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step10.html

Scheduled at https://www.ustraveldocs.com/tr/tr/step-2#how-to-apply-for-non-immigrant-visa. Requires payment of the MRV visa application fee (~$185). Applicants choose "Premium Cargo" for passport delivery after the interview and should print appointment instructions and payment receipts.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Visa Interview Required Documents
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step11.html

Required documents (22 total) include: valid passport (valid 6+ months beyond intended stay), Form I-20 with SEVIS ID, SEVIS fee receipt, visa appointment confirmation, DS-160 confirmation page, Firat University official transcript (English), SHSU unofficial transcript (from mysam.shsu.edu), CV/resume, bank statement (e.g., $39,110), sponsor's bank statement and proof of income, proof of ties to home country, MRV fee receipt, scholarship plan proof, university agreement protocol, course curriculum plan, acceptance letter, biometric photo, military status documentation (if applicable), Honors College acceptance letter (if applicable), and course schedule.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Visa Interview Questions
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step12.html

Typical interview topics: motivation for studying in the U.S. and choosing SHSU, academic program/background, financial sponsorship, family ties to America, post-graduation plans, work intentions, prior employment. Answers should match the DS-160 exactly, sound confident and clear (not rehearsed), and be given in English. If the visa is rejected, contact gec@shsu.edu.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Social Security Number Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step22.html

International students with an on-campus job offer apply for an SSN at the Social Security Administration office, 600 Sgt Ed Holcomb Blvd., Conroe, TX 77304 (Mon-Fri, 9:00 AM-3:00 PM). Required documents: completed Social Security Card Application, employment letter from the hiring department, letter from the Global Engagement Center (GEC), Form I-20, passport with valid F-1 visa, I-94 record, and birth certificate (if available). Students cannot apply for an SSN until physically present in the U.S. and after receiving hiring documents.

**Source:** SHSU International Students and Scholars Services (ISSS) / Global Engagement Center
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/offices-departments/global-engagement/international-students-and-scholars/

The Global Engagement Center (GEC) is the primary contact for F-1 international students, J-1 exchange students, and J-1 research scholars at SHSU, assisting with immigration rules and regulations and providing support services including initial airport pick-up and cultural activities. GEC is located in the Farrington Building, room 116 (email gec@shsu.edu, phone 936-294-4737).

Work authorization for F-1 students: Curricular Practical Training (CPT) is typically available after two full-time semesters of enrollment and must be tied to a course for academic credit. Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows F-1 students to work in their field of study for up to 12 months, usually after graduation, and requires DSO recommendation and USCIS approval.

**Source:** SHSU International Student Health Insurance (Academic Health Plans / myahpcare)
**URL:** https://shsu-intl.myahpcare.com/waiver

International students at SHSU are required to participate in the Student Health Insurance Plan administered through Academic Health Plans (myahpcare). Students who already have comparable coverage must submit a waiver/opt-out through the portal to decline the university plan.
