# Credit Transfer and Course Equivalency

**Source:** SHSU Admissions — Transfer Steps Checklist
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/admissions/apply/transfer-steps-checklist

SHSU accepts up to 70 transferable credit hours from accredited institutions. Transfer GPA requirements: 0-11 transferable hours requires meeting freshman requirements with a 2.5 GPA on transferable hours; 12-17 hours requires a 2.5 GPA; 18+ hours requires a 2.0 GPA. Students can use the Transfer Equivalency Self-Service (TESS) platform to view how their courses from other schools may apply to their SHSU degree. Transferring students have access to the EVOLVE transfer hub for additional resources and guidance throughout the transition.

**Source:** SHSU Undergraduate Catalog — Transfer of Credit / Core Curriculum
**URL:** https://catalog.shsu.edu/undergraduate/transfer-credit/

The Transfer Equivalency Self-Service (TESS) tool lets prospective and current students look up how courses from another institution transfer to SHSU. The SHSU Core Curriculum totals 42 semester credit hours across nine component areas. SHSU also publishes Transfer Course Equivalency Guides and Transfer Articulation pages (organized by subject, e.g., Computer Science, Business, Education) mapping Texas Common Course Number (TCCN) equivalents and specific partner-institution courses to SHSU courses.

**Source:** SHSU SACSCOC Reaffirmation — Standard 10.8, Evaluating and Awarding Academic Credit
**URL:** https://sacscoc.shsu.edu/2019/compliance-narratives/10.8.html

SHSU has an established 2+2 transfer articulation program with Firat University in Turkey: a bachelor's degree program at SHSU that includes 2 years of coursework completed at Firat University. Courses taken by students through this agreement are accepted by SHSU as transfer credit. As part of SACSCOC Standard 10.8 compliance, SHSU documents that its consideration of these international cooperative degree agreements includes ensuring qualified faculty, adequate learning resources, clear transfer policies, appropriate course equivalencies, consistent admission requirements, and ongoing assessment activities for the partner program.
