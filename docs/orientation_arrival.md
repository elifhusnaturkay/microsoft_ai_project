# Orientation and Arrival

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Flight Booking
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step14.html

Students must obtain a U.S. F-1 student visa before booking flights to Texas. Students may enter the U.S. up to 30 days before the program start date listed on the I-20. The nearest major airport is Houston George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH). Booking flights in advance typically yields better pricing and flexibility.

**Airport pick-up service:** SHSU provides complimentary airport transportation for newly arriving international students on a first-come, first-served basis. Restrictions: only for flights arriving at IAH, only on predetermined dates, flight must arrive before 4 p.m. to be eligible, and only for students arriving from outside the United States. Registration is through an online (Qualtrics) form.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — International Student Orientation
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step21.html

The Global Engagement Center requires all incoming international students to complete mandatory orientation with two components:

**In-person session:** Example date/time: August 22, 2025, 9:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m., at Farrington Building Room 105, 1908 Avenue J, Huntsville, TX 77341. Students must bring passport with visa, initial I-20 form, and I-94 record (obtainable from the CBP website after U.S. entry). Failure to check in at orientation with these documents may result in termination of the SEVIS record and F-1 status. Registration is via an online form.

**Virtual Blackboard modules:** Example timeline: modules open August 1, 2025, with a completion deadline of August 20, 2025 (modules typically open 15 days before in-person orientation). Login via SHSU Blackboard, navigate to "SHSU New International Student Orientation" under My Organizations. There are 12 modules total, each with a quiz requiring a minimum 80% score to advance. Incomplete modules by the deadline result in a registration hold.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Honors College Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step16.html

Students with an SHSU GPA above 3.25 may apply to the Honors College via the official application form using SHSU Self-Service Center credentials. Requirements: two letters of recommendation from recent college academic instructors (sent to honors@shsu.edu), an essay of 500-1000 words, and a résumé covering leadership, employment, awards, and volunteer work. Benefits include financial support opportunities, community engagement, peer networking, specialized coursework, reduced class sizes, early registration, an Honors Lounge and Lab, and an honors diploma distinction. Graduation requirements: maintain 3.25 cumulative GPA, complete honors advising each semester, earn 18 hours in honors courses/contracts, complete 6 hours of honors seminars, complete 10 community service hours per semester (to maintain early registration eligibility), and attend 3+ honors events annually. "Highest Honors" requires a senior thesis under faculty supervision. New students complete the Honors Participation Agreement Form, register for orientation, and schedule advising through Navigate 360.
