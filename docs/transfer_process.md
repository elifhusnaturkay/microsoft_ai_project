# Transfer Process (Firat University to SHSU) — Step by Step

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide (reference site built for Firat-SHSU transfer students)
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/

This is a sequential, step-by-step guide (steps 0-25) covering the full transfer application journey from English proficiency testing through visa interview. Steps are summarized in order below.

## Step 0: Duolingo English Test
Applicants without an approved English proficiency test score can take the Duolingo English Test as an alternative. Official site: englishtest.duolingo.com. The test can be taken online from home using a computer with a camera, microphone, and internet access. It takes roughly one hour and includes a brief interview and writing components. Results are usually available within 48 hours. Minimum required score: 100. Tips: practice in advance using free resources on the Duolingo website, ensure a quiet well-lit room with no interruptions, and follow all rules strictly since violations may result in score invalidation.

## Step 1: ApplyTexas Account and Application
Create an account at ApplyTexas.org (https://www.applytexas.org/). On the homepage, select "Create an Account" and enter name, email, password, and other required information. Log in, click "Add a New Application," and for application type choose "International Transfer." Complete the full application (personal details, academic history, term information) and submit. Check email for an application confirmation message.

## Step 2: SHSU Self-Service Center
Access the SHSU Self-Service Center at https://shsu.radiusbycampusmgmt.com/ssc/zx671c700x6G0x67020C673.ssc to manage your account. Create a new account and log in. After a few days, upload sections appear in the application section.

## Step 3: Document Upload
Get your first transcript from OBS and upload it to the SHSU Self-Service Center. Send your Duolingo English test score to Sam Houston State University (Undergraduate Admissions). Complete payment of the $90 application fee. Send correspondence to admissions@shsu.edu with attached documents, specifying your status as a transfer student from Firat University.

## Step 4: SHSU Email Activation
After accepting an SHSU offer, you receive an email with your SAM username and activation instructions. Activate at https://account.shsu.edu/legacy/Activation. You must set your password following the instructions in the acceptance email. Email format: [SAM username]@shsu.edu.

## Step 5: Duo Two-Factor Authentication
After activating your SHSU email, set up Duo two-factor authentication at https://duo.shsu.edu — enroll your device and complete the security setup. This is required for accessing SHSU resources securely.

## Step 6: I-20 Application — Document Upload
Requires bank statement proof of at least $39,110 USD available in the account, and a clear photo/scan of the passport information page. If funds come from someone else's account, a signed sponsor letter is required (a sample sponsor letter, sponsor.pdf, is provided); if the account belongs to the applicant with sufficient funds, no sponsor letter is needed. Upload options appear in the application portal within days to a week after email activation. Email completed documents to lah050@shsu.edu including your SAM ID.

## Step 7: TB Questionnaire and Meningitis Vaccine
Students receive their I-20 via email one to two weeks after submitting documents. Access the SHSU Patient Portal using SAM credentials, provide date of birth, and go to "Forms and Clearances" to complete the "TB Risk Screen" questionnaire. For the meningitis vaccine: download and print the vaccination form, have a physician complete and sign it after administering the vaccine, and upload it to the Patient Portal under "Forms and Clearances" > "Immunization Records," recording vaccine type and exact administration date in the "Meningococcal ACWY" section. Vaccination can be obtained at a local Halk Sağlığı Merkezi or Sağlık Ocağı.

## Step 8: SEVIS Fee Payment
The SEVIS (I-901) fee is $350 and must be paid before starting the visa application. Pay at fmjfee.com/i901fee/index.html using the SEVIS ID number found on the I-20. Download and retain the confirmation receipt — it is needed at the visa appointment.

## Step 9: DS-160 Form
Complete the DS-160 Nonimmigrant Visa Application at https://ceac.state.gov/genniv/. Select your visa interview location, start a new application, choose DS-160, and copy/save your application ID number immediately. Indicate visa type F-1. Enter SHSU's address: 1905 University Ave, Huntsville, TX 77340, USA. Provide contact details for the U.S. contact person (the staff member who sent the I-20). Download and retain the confirmation page.

## Step 10: Visa Appointment Scheduling
Schedule at https://www.ustraveldocs.com/tr/tr/step-2#how-to-apply-for-non-immigrant-visa. Create a new account and follow the steps. Pay the MRV visa application fee (~$185). Choose a preferred date/time. Select "Premium Cargo" for passport delivery to your address after the interview. Print appointment instructions and payment receipts for the interview day.

## Step 11: Visa Interview — Required Documents
22 documents are listed, including: valid passport (valid at least 6 months beyond intended stay), Form I-20, SEVIS fee receipt, visa appointment confirmation printout, DS-160 confirmation page, Firat University official transcript (in English), SHSU unofficial transcript (from mysam.shsu.edu), CV/resume, bank statement showing sufficient funds (e.g., $39,110), sponsor's bank statement and proof of income/employment, proof of ties to home country (property, family business, work intention letter), MRV fee receipt, scholarship plan proof (from Financial Aid Dashboard offers section), university agreement protocol (protocol.pdf), course curriculum plan (course.png), acceptance letter from the SHSU Self-Service Center, biometric visa-compliant photo, military status documentation if applicable, Honors College acceptance letter if accepted, and course schedule for the intended semester.

## Step 12: Visa Interview Questions
Typical interview questions cover motivation for studying in the U.S. and choice of SHSU, academic program and background, financial sponsorship details, family ties to America, post-graduation plans, work intentions, and previous employment history. Guidance: answers should match the DS-160 form exactly; show certainty and clarity without sounding rehearsed; speak in English throughout (the visa officer likely doesn't speak Turkish); avoid excessive requests to repeat questions. If rejected, contact gec@shsu.edu.

**Source:** SHSU Admissions — Transfer Steps Checklist
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/admissions/apply/transfer-steps-checklist

Transfer students must: (1) submit an application through ApplyTexas or the Common App and pay the application fee; (2) send official transcripts from all previously attended colleges/universities (SHSU school code: 003606); (3) know the deadlines — Fall: August 1, Spring: December 15, Summer: May 15.

Transfer GPA requirements by hours completed: 0-11 transferable hours requires meeting freshman requirements with a 2.5 GPA on transferable hours; 12-17 hours requires a 2.5 GPA; 18+ hours requires a 2.0 GPA. SHSU accepts up to 70 transferable credit hours from accredited institutions. Students can use the Transfer Equivalency Self-Service (TESS) platform to view how courses from other schools may apply to their SHSU degree. Transferring students also have access to the EVOLVE transfer hub for resources and guidance.
