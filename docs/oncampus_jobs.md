# On-Campus Jobs and Career Services

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — On-Campus Work Application Guide
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step17.html

International undergraduate and graduate students at SHSU can work on campus during the academic year. Two job portals: primary is PeopleAdmin (shsu.peopleadmin.com); secondary is Aramark.com (search "Sam Houston" — opens later in the term). Applicants can authenticate via LinkedIn, upload resumes/transcripts to their profile, browse the "Search Jobs" section, and submit applications.

**Work hour restrictions:** Fall/Spring — up to 20 hours per week. Summer — up to 28 hours per week, if enrollment requirements are met.

**Post-hiring requirements:** Two letters are needed — one from the employing department (job title, start date, supervisor name) and one from the Global Engagement Center (confirming F-1 status, SAM ID, SEVIS number). Final steps: apply for a Social Security Number, submit documentation to Human Resources, and complete course registration.

---

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Social Security Number Application
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step22.html

Students with an on-campus job offer apply for an SSN at the Social Security Administration office, 600 Sgt Ed Holcomb Blvd., Conroe, TX 77304 (Mon-Fri, 9:00 AM-3:00 PM). Required documents: completed Social Security Card Application, employment letter from the hiring department, letter from the Global Engagement Center, Form I-20, passport with valid F-1 visa, I-94 record, and birth certificate (if available). Students cannot apply for an SSN until physically present in the U.S. and after receiving hiring documents.

**Source:** SHSU Career Success Center
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/offices-departments/career-success-center/

The Career Success Center offers services to both current students and alumni, including resume and cover letter reviews, mock interviews, career conversations, and job/internship search support, plus access to the "Bearkats Get Hired" LinkedIn group. Students get 24/7 access to job postings, resources, and career-readiness modules through the Blackboard Learning Lounge. Resume critiques from career coaches take 3-5 business days when requested by email. Contact: careersuccesscenter@shsu.edu, phone 936-294-4454, located at AB 4, Suite 210, hours Monday-Friday 8 AM-5 PM.
