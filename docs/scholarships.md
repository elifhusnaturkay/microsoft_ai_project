# Scholarships and Financial Aid

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — SHSU Internal Scholarships
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step15.html

Students apply for internal scholarships after submitting their Honors College application (if applicable), via the scholarship portal at https://shsu.academicworks.com (log in with SAM account credentials, go to "Our Opportunities," review matching options, submit required documentation before deadlines).

**Firat University special scholarship:** In early June, students must complete the Firat University Scholarship Form and send it to both mkarabatak@firat.edu.tr and pdmartin@shsu.edu. Afterward, applicants should complete the general application through Scholarships4Kats. **Source:** SHSU Cost and Financial Aid — Scholarships
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/cost-aid/types-of-aid/scholarships

All SHSU scholarship applications go through the Scholarships4Kats portal. Students must be accepted to SHSU before applying. The general application opens each September, with a priority deadline of November 1st for the following academic year. Notifications are typically sent by May 1st.

**Bearkat Transfer Scholarship:** $2,000 total ($1,000 Fall, $1,000 Spring). Automatic — no separate application required beyond admission. Eligibility: first-time SHSU students enrolling full-time in Fall, transferring from an accredited institution with 30-89 transferable credit hours, minimum 2.75 GPA. Spring transfers are not eligible.

**National Merit Scholarship:** $40,000 total ($10,000/year over 4 years). Requires National Merit Scholarship Program finalist status and designating SHSU as first college choice by May 31st.

**Smith-Hutson Scholarship:** For students with demonstrated financial need and academic potential; available to Texas high school graduates and Texas community college transfers. Award amounts vary annually.

**Honors Scholarship:** Up to $40,000 over an undergraduate career (maximum 4 years).

**SHSU Local Scholarship:** $1,000 annually for 4 years, for graduating seniors from eight specified Texas counties.

**Non-Resident Tuition Waiver:** Students who receive at least $1,000 from a single institutional competitive scholarship may qualify for a non-resident tuition waiver matching the scholarship's period of coverage — converting nonresident (international) tuition rates to in-state rates for that period.

**Source:** SHSU Cost and Financial Aid — Bearkat Emergency Fund
**URL:** https://www.shsu.edu/cost-aid/bearkats-helping-bearkats

The Bearkat Emergency Fund gives temporary financial assistance to students facing emergencies (medical expenses, loss of income, food insecurity), administered by the Financial Aid and Scholarships Office. The related Bearkats Helping Bearkats initiative and Bearkat Assistance Program provide confidential access to staff and resources for students in financial crisis.
