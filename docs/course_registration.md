# Course Registration and Academic Advising

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — TSI Exam
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step13.html

The Texas Success Initiative (TSI) Assessment is a mandatory evaluation for certain students to determine readiness for college-level coursework. Process: (1) complete a Pre-Assessment Activity (PAA) before exam registration; (2) after finishing the PAA, schedule the TSI test session. Some students may qualify for exemptions based on standardized test scores or educational background — exemption eligibility can be reviewed and exemption forms submitted. TSI results affect course placement and are tied to institutional policy.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Class Registration
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step18.html

Before registering for classes, students must complete four items: (1) schedule and attend an academic advisor appointment (booked via campus.eab.com); (2) complete the TSI Exam; (3) submit a TB Questionnaire; (4) provide proof of Meningitis Vaccine.

**Registration process:**
1. Meet with an advisor to discuss course selection based on program and academic standing.
2. Access the registration portal at samweb.shsu.edu using SHSU credentials — address pre-registration notices, review registration holds, accept required informational items, and select courses in the Add/Drop Classes section.

**Tuition and payment:** Payment can occur before departure or after arrival in Texas via the Global Engagement Center. Payment plan enrollment is available through a secure portal (secure.touchnet.com). Scholarships apply on August 15th. Students may opt out of the Bearkat Bundle textbook service (via samweb.shsu.edu) if done at least 30 days before the class start date.

General guidance: register early for the best course availability; consider checking instructor ratings on RateMyProfessors.com.

**Source:** SHSU Transfer Process Guide — Title IX / Campus Prevention Training
**URL:** https://umawi1427.github.io/shsu-transfer-process/step19.html

All first-time, first-year, transfer, and graduate students must complete the online Campus Prevention Training to avoid a registration hold. The training takes approximately 2 hours and can be completed across multiple sessions at the student's own pace (recommended: start early and complete sections in increments). Training becomes available one week after course registration through the SHSU training portal: https://shsustudents-tx.vectorlmsedu.com/training/home. Registration holds related to this training can also be viewed through this portal. Contact for questions/technical issues: tixhold@shsu.edu.
